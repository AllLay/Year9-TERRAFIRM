Get to Know Our Team

Meet the Team Behind TERRAFIRM.

Three students, one shared mission. We came together to explore how natural disasters affect Myanmar and the communities living in it.

Through research, creativity, and teamwork, we developed TERRAFIRM to raise awareness, encourage disaster preparedness, and help promote a safer and more resilient future.

Our Background

Our Story

Every project begins with an idea. TERRAFIRM began as a shared effort to create something informative, useful and meaningful.

The Idea

We wanted to create a project that goes beyond a normal school assignment and communicates information in a meaningful way.

The Team

By combining our ideas, skills and efforts, we worked together to plan and develop the TERRAFIRM project.

The Result

Our ideas developed into a website that brings together research, information and educational resources.

Get to Know Our Team

Meet the Team Behind TERRAFIRM.

Three students, one shared mission. We came together to explore how natural disasters affect Myanmar and the communities living in it.

Through research, creativity, and teamwork, we developed TERRAFIRM to raise awareness, encourage disaster preparedness, and help promote a safer and more resilient future.

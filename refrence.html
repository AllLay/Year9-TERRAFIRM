TERRAFIRM RESEARCH

References & Sources

Explore the research, reports and official information used in TERRAFIRM to understand natural disasters and their impacts on Myanmar's people, environment and communities.

Floods

Flood research and reports

Research and reports about flooding, its causes, affected communities, environmental damage and flood preparedness in Myanmar.

United Nations - OCHA
Myanmar Flood Situation Report - 16 September 2024

Documents the September 2024 floods caused by heavy monsoon rainfall and the remnants of Typhoon Yagi.

World Bank
Myanmar Floods and Landslides - Post Disaster Needs Assessment

Examines the damage, economic losses and social impacts of the 2015 floods and landslides in Myanmar.

Myanmar Department of Meteorology and Hydrology
Official Weather and Flood Information

Provides weather forecasts, rainfall information, flood warnings and other meteorological updates.

Earthquakes

Seismic activity and earthquake research

Research about earthquake causes, tectonic activity, seismic hazards, historical earthquakes and their impacts on Myanmar.

United States Geological Survey (USGS)
2025 Magnitude 7.7 Mandalay Earthquake Research

Scientific research explaining the 2025 Mandalay earthquake, its rupture along the Sagaing Fault and its geological characteristics.

United Nations - UN Myanmar
Initial Situation Report: Central Myanmar Earthquake

Reports the impacts of the 28 March 2025 earthquake, including casualties, building collapses and infrastructure damage.

Myanmar Department of Meteorology and Hydrology
Official Earthquake Information

Provides official earthquake information, seismic updates and related monitoring services.

Landslides

Landslide hazards and research

Research about landslide causes, rainfall-triggered slope failures, environmental impacts and landslide risks in mountainous areas of Myanmar.

World Bank
Myanmar Floods and Landslides - Post Disaster Needs Assessment

Examines the impacts of the 2015 floods and landslides, including damage to homes, agriculture and infrastructure.

United Nations - UN Myanmar
Hpakant Landslide - 2 July 2020

Reports on the deadly landslide at a jade mining site in Hpakant, Kachin State, and its human impacts.

Myanmar Information Management Unit (MIMU)
Chin State Floods and Landslides: Community-led Response and Assessment

Provides an assessment of flood and landslide impacts in several Chin State townships during 2015.

Droughts

Drought research and water scarcity

Research about drought causes, water shortages, agricultural impacts, climate conditions and sustainable water management in Myanmar.

United Nations - UN ESCAP
Ready for the Dry Years: Building Resilience to Drought in Asia and the Pacific

Examines drought conditions in Myanmar's Central Dry Zone, including the severe dry spells during 2014 and 2015.

United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)
Addressing Climate Change Risks on Water Resources and Food Security in Myanmar's Dry Zone

Explores climate risks, water resource management and food security in Myanmar's Central Dry Zone.

Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)
Myanmar Drought Information System

Provides drought monitoring maps, drought indicators and information about drought conditions throughout Myanmar.

Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)
Myanmar Agricultural Drought Monitoring and Historical Frequency

Provides satellite-based agricultural drought information and historical drought-frequency maps.

About These References

These references include official reports, scientific research, disaster assessments and monitoring resources from international organisations and Myanmar government departments. They are used to support the information presented throughout the TERRAFIRM website.

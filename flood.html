FLOODS

When water rises, preparation saves lives. Understand the dangers of floods and learn how communities can stay safe and prepared.

Understanding Floods

What Is A Flood?

Floods occur due to excessive amounts of water being in areas where water should not normally be present. The causes of flooding include rainfall, overflowing river, storm surge, and bad drainage system.

Floods are usually very dangerous to properties, transportation systems, and even human lives. They can occur suddenly or gradually after several days.

Why It Happens

Main Causes Of Flooding

Understanding the causes helps us reduce flood risks.

Heavy Rainfall

Continuous or intense rainfall can cause rivers and drainage systems to overflow.

Poor Drainage Systems

Blocked or poorly designed drains prevent rainwater from flowing away properly.

Deforestation

Removing trees reduces water absorption and increases surface runoff.

The Consequences

Effects Of Floods

Flooding affects both people and the environment.

🏠

Property Damage

Homes, buildings, and infrastructure can be destroyed.

🌱

Environmental Damage

Floods can damage ecosystems and agricultural land.

🦠

Health Risks

Contaminated water can spread diseases and infections.

🚧

Transportation Problems

Roads and bridges may become unsafe or inaccessible.

Be Prepared

How Can We Reduce Flood Risks?

Small actions and long-term solutions can make a big difference in protecting communities from floods.

01. Maintain Drainage Systems

Ensure that the drains, canals, and waterways are free of rubbish and other debris to allow rainwater to drain effectively. Proper drainage ensures that there will be no water accumulation in residential areas due to heavy rains since water will be draining from residential areas.

02. Plant More Trees and Protect Forests

Tree planting and protection of forests will ensure that soil is maintained and the rate of surface runoff is minimized to the lowest possible level. Trees and other vegetation will ensure that water flowing into the rivers is minimized.

03. Improve Flood Protection Infrastructure

The most essential thing will be the building of proper drainage system, flood barriers, breakwater and retention ponds for the effective control of excess water and river overflow.

04. Raise Public Awareness

As floods impact people differently, it will be necessary to educate people about the risks of floods, warning signs, and the measures to be taken in case of floods. It is necessary to inform people about the weather forecasting source and the measures to be taken in case of floods and evacuation points in case of dangerous floods.

05. Develop Early Warning Systems

It will be necessary to warn people about the risk of floods through mobile phones, radios and community messages based on the weather forecast, river level and emergency warnings. It is necessary to have a bag which will have some of the essentials like water, food, flashlight, batteries, first aid and important documents.

06. Prepare Emergency Supplies and Evacuation Plans

Waterproof bags should be used to store things like water, food, flashlights, batteries, first aid kit, and other relevant items. It is also advisable that you know where to head to when there is a flood.

Stay Safe

Flood Safety Guide

Know what to do before, during, and after a flood.

Before A Flood

  • Monitor weather forecasts and warnings.
  • Prepare an emergency kit.
  • Know your evacuation routes.
  • Keep important documents protected.
  • Move valuable items to higher places.

During A Flood

  • Follow official emergency instructions.
  • Move to higher ground when advised.
  • Stay away from floodwater.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Switch off electricity if safe to do so.

After A Flood

  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid damaged buildings and electrical wires.
  • Drink safe and clean water.
  • Clean and disinfect affected areas safely.
  • Report dangerous conditions to authorities.

Awareness Today. Safety Tomorrow.

Floods cannot always be prevented, but their impacts can be reduced through awareness, preparation, and effective community action.

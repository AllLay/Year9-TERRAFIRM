WHY PREPARATION MATTERS

Preparation starts before the disaster.

Disaster preparation is not only about reacting when something happens. Understanding risks early can help people make safer decisions and protect their communities and environment.

ABOUT TERRAFIRM

Awareness today. Resilience tomorrow.

TERRAFIRM is a Year 9 Global Perspectives project exploring how natural disasters affect Myanmar's environment and people. Our goal is to increase awareness, encourage preparation and promote long-term resilience.
